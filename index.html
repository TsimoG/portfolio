                  Thesis

                  Bachelor's thesis · 2026

                  Desert Levels in Video Games: What significance does instrumentation and mode have for the listener's perception and associations of desert environments in video game music?

                  Original title (Swedish)Ökennivåer i spel: Vilken betydelse har instrumentering respektive skala för lyssnarens upplevelse och associationer till ökenmiljöer i spelmusik?

                  Game music often leans on cultural conventions to tell the player where they are. We composed four pieces that differ only in instrumentation and scale, played one piece to each of four listener groups, and surveyed how they experienced the setting, what they felt and which places it made them think of. The sample was small, so the study works as a pilot for a larger follow-up.

                  About

                  I'm a composer, sound engineer and game developer based in Vantaa, Finland. In 2026 I graduated from the University of Skövde with a Bachelor's degree in Game Development, specialising in music. I composed the music for Fish Isle and Windward, and on Manhattan Flatline I was both composer and in charge of the game's audio. My first game, Capsule Rush, I made entirely on my own and released on Steam. I'm curious, love learning new things and enjoy working with others. I speak Swedish and English fluently, and some Finnish. Right now I'm looking for my next opportunity in games and audio.
